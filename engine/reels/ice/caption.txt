# Why ice floats: frozen water locks into open hexagonal rings, so the same water takes more room and weighs less per litre.
== hook | ground: dark
Almost every *solid*
sinks in its own *liquid.*
*Ice* floats,
with nine *tenths* of it
under the *water.*
== lattice | ground: dark
As water *freezes,*
each molecule locks onto
*four* neighbours,
in *rings* of six
with *space* in the middle.
== weight | ground: dark
The same water now
takes up more *room,*
so a litre of ice *weighs* less
than a litre of *water.*
That's why *lakes* freeze
from the *top* down.
