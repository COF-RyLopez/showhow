When you dial nine-one-one, a map decides who answers. Next generation nine-one-one sets out exactly how that map is built.

Address points and road centerlines say where you are. Each stretch of road carries its house numbers, left side and right.

Call center boundaries pick who answers. Police, fire and medical boundaries pick who comes.

Every feature has its own unique ID, and every layer uses the same GPS coordinates, so neighboring counties join into one map.
