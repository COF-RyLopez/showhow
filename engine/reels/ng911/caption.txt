# The NG9-1-1 GIS data model (NENA-STA-006.3): the layers that route a 9-1-1 call and send help.
== hook | ground: dark
When you dial *nine-one-one,*
a *map* decides who answers.
Next generation nine-one-one
sets out exactly
how that map is *built.*
== where | ground: dark
Address *points*
and road *centerlines*
say where you are.
Each stretch of *road*
carries its house *numbers,*
*left* side
and *right.*
== who | ground: dark
Call center *boundaries*
pick who *answers.*
*Police,* fire and medical boundaries
pick who *comes.*
== join | ground: dark
Every feature has
its own unique *ID,*
and every layer uses
the same GPS *coordinates,*
so neighboring *counties*
join into one *map.*
