# Gears: 10 teeth drive 30, a third of a turn per turn, the other way, three times the force.
== hook | ground: dark
A small gear / with *ten* teeth
can turn a big one
with *thirty.*
== ratio | ground: dark
Each full *turn*
of the small gear
moves the big gear
only a *third* of a turn,
the other *way.*
== force | ground: dark
It trades *speed*
for turning *force,*
so the big gear pushes
*three* times as hard.
